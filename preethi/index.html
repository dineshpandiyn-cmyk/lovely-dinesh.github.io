Author bio

Short bio

Preethi Nagarethinam is a psychology professional, educator and content writer. She holds an MA in Psychology, an MA in English Literature and a B.Ed., and writes for Psychologs Mental Health Magazine, turning complex psychological ideas into accessible content for diverse audiences.

Full bio

Preethi Nagarethinam is a psychologist, educator and content writer. She holds an MA in Psychology, an MA in English Literature and a B.Ed. As a writer for Psychologs Mental Health Magazine, she writes and edits articles and blogs on mental health and psychology, simplifies complex ideas for diverse audiences and works with editorial teams to promote awareness and reduce stigma. She is also a life skills and POCSO Act trainer who has trained more than 5,000 students through skill development programmes in colleges across Tamil Nadu. She has published over 50 poems and compiled two books.
